# Build a trade business website

## When to use

- Someone asks for a website for a plumber, septic, electrician, HVAC, roofer or other trades business.
- Someone wants to change a website preview they already made.
- Someone asks what it costs to keep the site.

## Steps

1. Get the business name, phone, city and the real services they offer.
2. If they have photos or a logo they own, upload them with sdwb_upload_image.
3. Build the preview with sdwb_create_site. Share the live link right away.
4. Make changes with sdwb_update_site when asked.
5. For price, call sdwb_get_pricing. Do not make up prices.
6. When the owner wants to keep the site, give them the link from sdwb_get_claim_link. No payment happens in chat.

## Rules

- Use only real facts the owner gives you. Do not invent reviews, licenses or awards.
- Only use photos the owner has the right to use.
